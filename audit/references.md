# Metric audit source quality

Every scientific claim in a completed metric record must identify the source
that supports it. References should use the strongest source available:

- `primary`: original research that introduced or formally defined the metric.
- `authoritative`: a standard, official technical specification, or maintained
  documentation from the responsible organization or project.
- `secondary`: a review, textbook, or other synthesis used only when no primary
  or authoritative source can be located.

Each citation must include a stable URL or DOI, title, authors or responsible
organization, year when available, and a concise statement of the exact claim
it supports. Implementation-derived observations must be labeled as such and
must not be presented as source-backed scientific facts. Unknown or disputed
facts remain explicit rather than inferred.
